## 1. About the Company
**Techashi Solutions Ltd.** is an end-to-end technology solutions company providing enterprise-grade IT products and managed services to individuals, small-and-medium enterprises (SMEs), and corporate institutions across Kenya and East Africa. 

We bridge the gap between premium hardware procurement and certified IT engineering: whether an individual needs a tested refurbished business laptop, an office needs high-speed structured networking and 4K CCTV surveillance, or a growing company needs seamless Microsoft 365 cloud migration, Techashi is their one-stop, trusted technology partner.

* **Primary Tagline:** *"Smart Tech. Reliable Solutions."*
* **Secondary / Marketing Tagline:** *"Your Trusted Single-Source IT & Hardware Partner."*
* **Core Brand Archetype:** Competent, Agile, Trustworthy, Modern, Tech-Forward.

## 2. Website Goals & Strategic KPIs
1. **Corporate Credibility & Authority:** Mirror the polished, professional credibility of industry leaders (clean corporate aesthetic, trust badges, verified metrics, certified partner badges).
2. **High-Velocity Lead Generation:** Seamless frictionless capture points on every fold — Click-to-WhatsApp, direct phone dialer, instant quote requests, and an interactive multi-service inquiry form.
3. **Dual-Funnel Engagement:**
   - **B2B Funnel:** Corporate procurement managers seeking IT consultancy, office networking, CCTV installations, M365 licensing, and AMC maintenance contracts.
   - **B2C / SME Funnel:** Students, professionals, and small business owners looking to browse, compare, and order new/refurbished PCs, monitors, and gadgets with clear warranty terms.
4. **Social Proof & Assurance:** Overcome buyer hesitation with transparent warranty statements (3–12 months on hardware), verified customer testimonials, and photos of real server rack & networking installations.

## 6. Key Features & Functional Requirements
* **Mobile-First Responsive Layout:** >75% of Kenyan users browse via mobile; all buttons, tables, and product cards must be thumb-friendly.
* **Instant Omnichannel Lead Capture:**
  - Floating WhatsApp widget pre-filled with: *"Hello Techashi, I'd like to inquire about [Service/Product]..."*
  - Direct click-to-call `tel:` links on all phone mentions.
* **Quotation & Estimator Engine:** Lead forms automatically send email notifications to `info@techashi.co.ke` and generate a confirmation SMS/email to the client.
* **Product Catalog Flexibility:** Dual pricing display: explicit price (e.g. `KSh 38,500`) with quick option for *"Call for Bulk Corporate Pricing"*.
* **Payment Integration:**
  - Primary: **Lipa Na M-Pesa** (Paybill & Till Number integration via Daraja API or payment gateway).
  - Secondary: Visa, Mastercard, and Direct Bank EFT for corporate invoices.
* **Performance & Speed:** Sub-2 second load times on 4G, WebP image compression, clean semantic HTML5 / Next.js / Tailwind or modern WordPress (Elementor Pro/Kadence).
* **Security & Compliance:** Valid SSL certificate (HTTPS), Cloudflare CDN & DDoS protection, automated daily backups.
* **Content Management (Admin Panel):** Intuitive dashboard allowing non-technical staff to add new products, adjust pricing, update stock status, and post blog articles without developer intervention.

## 8. Design Style & Visual Identity System

### Color Palette (Extracted Precisely from Brand Identity)
* **Primary Tech Navy Blue:** `#00529B` *(Represents authority, security, corporate stability, and tech depth — used in headers, primary branding, and text).*
* **Accent Innovation Green:** `#72BF44` *(Represents energy, modern tech, vitality, and successful execution — used for primary CTA buttons, badges, highlights, and active states).*
* **Dark Contrast / Slate Navy:** `#071B2F` to `#0B2545` *(Hero backdrop, dark section backgrounds, and high-tech contrast panels).*
* **Pure Clean White:** `#FFFFFF` *(Card backgrounds, primary reading canvas, hero typography on dark panels).*
* **Soft Tech Slate / Light Gray:** `#F8FAFC` to `#F1F5F9` *(Alternating section backgrounds to ensure clean visual separation).*
* **Deep Charcoal Body Text:** `#1E293B` *(High readability, WCAG AAA compliant against white).*
* **Secondary Action Amber (Optional Warm Accent):** `#F59E0B` *(For special promotion badges and urgent alerts).*

### Typography
* **Primary Headings:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` or `Inter` (Bold 700 / Semi-Bold 600) — clean geometric tech styling.
* **Body Text:** `Inter` or `DM Sans` (Regular 400 / Medium 500) — optimal legibility across mobile displays.
